Rome

Dear friends,

I have the immense joy of announcing to you that, God willing, on Saturday, November 21, 2026 I will be ordained to the diaconate, and on Saturday, May 22, 2027, to the priesthood in Rome.

This is a step that fills me with deep gratitude to God, to the Church, and to so many people—family members, friends, mentors, and professors—who have accompanied me throughout these years in Spain and Italy.

This website is designed to provide you with all practical details regarding travel, ceremonies, precise locations, and other events in Rome. Above all, I ask of you from now on the most precious and indispensable gift: your constant prayers so that we may be good, holy priests, dedicated entirely to souls following in the footsteps of Jesus Christ.

To assist with planning and logistics, please confirm your attendance well in advance using the form at the bottom of this page or via the external RSVP link.

    Helpful Information

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers to common practical questions regarding the ceremonies in Rome.

    ✟
    What do I need to travel to Italy from Kenya?

    If you are not a citizen of a European country, you need a Schengen short-stay visa. You can check the specific requirements on the Italian Embassy website (ambnairobi.esteri.it). Given that the process can take weeks or months, I recommend that you start the booking for the VFS and embassy appointment as early as possible.

    What time should we arrive at the Basilica?

    The basilica doors open at 9:00 AM. It is recommended to arrive between 9:00 AM and 9:30 AM to find your seats and read the liturgical booklet before the procession begins at 10:00 AM.

    What is the recommended dress code?

    Formal / festive liturgical attire (suit and tie for men, elegant dress or suit for women with covered shoulders according to Roman basilica norms).

    Can children attend the ceremony?

    Yes, family members of all ages are welcome. Please note the solemn liturgy lasts approximately two and a half hours.
