A Word from Felix

Dear family & friends,

For years I have tried to find the right words to thank you, and I keep coming back to the simplest ones. You prayed for me when I did not know what I was asking for. You fed me, listened to me, and quietly asked how my studies were going — even when the answer was long.

Twice in the coming months I will lie prostrate on the floor of a church while the whole Church calls on the saints: first to be ordained a deacon, and then, God willing, a priest. I will not be lying there alone. Every one of you who has carried me in prayer will be there too.

Please come if you can. If you cannot, pray for me — and know that at my first Mass, and at every Mass after it, I will remember you at the altar.

In Christ,
Felix

The Road to the Altar

A vocation, slowly heard

✟

  1. Baptism

    Claimed for Christ

    Brought to the font by parents and godparents who promised to raise him in the faith — a promise they kept.

  2. The first stirring

    A question that would not go away

    A quiet sense, returning again and again in prayer, that God was asking for everything — and a friend who once asked, simply, “Have you ever thought about it?”

  3. Formation

    Years of preparation

    Philosophy, theology, work, and many hours before the Blessed Sacrament learning to listen.

  4. 21 November 2026

    Ordained to serve

    Promising prayer, celibacy and obedience, he receives the Book of the Gospels and becomes a deacon — a servant of the Word, the altar and the poor.

  5. 22 May 2027

    Tu es sacerdos in æternum

    “You are a priest for ever, in the line of Melchizedek.” His hands are anointed, and the next morning he offers his first Mass.

IIIRite of Ordination

Election of the Candidate

The candidate is called by name.

℣. Let Felix, who is to be ordained deaconpriest, come forward.

℟. Present.

A priest testifies that the candidate has been found worthy. The Bishop then chooses him for the Order of the DiaconatePriesthood.

℟. Thanks be to God.

The congregation shows its assent — traditionally by applause.

Homily

The Bishop preaches to the people and to the one to be ordained.

Promises of the Elect

The elect stands before the Bishop, who questions him on his resolve to serve the Church with humble charity, to hold the mystery of faith with a clear conscience, to remain celibate for the sake of the Kingdom, to pray the Liturgy of the Hours for the whole world, and to conform his life to Christ. To each he answers:

The elect stands before the Bishop, who questions him on his resolve to preach, to celebrate the sacraments, to pray for the people entrusted to him, and to be united ever more closely to Christ. To each he answers:

℟. I do.  /  I do, with the help of God.

Kneeling, he places his joined hands between those of the Bishop.

℣. Do you promise respect and obedience to me and my successors?

℟. I do.

℣. May God who has begun the good work in you bring it to fulfilment.

The Essential Moment

Laying on of Hands & Prayer of Ordination

In complete silence the Bishop alone lays his hands on the head of the elect, then sings the Prayer of Ordination. By this gesture and these words, Felix becomes a deacon.

In complete silence the Bishop lays his hands on the head of the elect. Every priest present then does the same, one by one. The Bishop sings the Prayer of Ordination; by this gesture and these words, Felix becomes a priest.

Please remain kneeling and silent — this is the heart of the day.

Explanatory Rites

Investiture
He is vested in the deacon’s stole, worn across the body, and the dalmatic.
Handing on of the Book of the Gospels
“Receive the Gospel of Christ, whose herald you have become. Believe what you read, teach what you believe, and practise what you teach.”
Fraternal Kiss
The Bishop, and then the deacons present, welcome their new brother with the sign of peace.
Investiture
He is vested in the priestly stole and chasuble.
Anointing of Hands
The Bishop anoints his palms with Sacred Chrism: “The Lord Jesus Christ, whom the Father anointed with the Holy Spirit and power, guard and preserve you, that you may sanctify the Christian people and offer sacrifice to God.”
Handing on of Bread & Wine
“Receive the oblation of the holy people, to be offered to God. Understand what you do, imitate what you celebrate, and conform your life to the mystery of the Lord’s Cross.”
Fraternal Kiss
The Bishop, and then the priests, welcome their new brother into the presbyterate with the sign of peace.
IVLiturgy of the Eucharist

For the first time the new deacon serves at the altar: he receives the gifts, prepares the chalice, and ministers the Precious Blood at Communion.

For the first time, the new priest stands at the altar with the Bishop and concelebrates the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass.

Catholics in a state of grace are invited to receive Holy Communion. All others are warmly welcome to come forward for a blessing with arms crossed over the chest, or to remain in prayer.

VConcluding Rites

The Bishop imparts the solemn blessing. Then, for the first time, the new deacon dismisses the people.

℣. Go forth, the Mass is ended.

℟. Thanks be to God.

After Mass, Felix will give his first priestly blessings. It is a long and happy tradition to receive one — and to kiss his newly anointed hands.

Planning your visit

i.

Arriving

Ordinations draw a full church. Please plan to be seated 30 minutes early; the front pews are kept for family and clergy, and ushers will guide you.

ii.

Getting there

Venues and directions are on each celebration above. Car-sharing is warmly encouraged.

iii.

What to wear

Sunday best. Clergy attending are invited to vest; please bring your own alb and white stole.

iv.

Photographs

We ask that guests keep phones away during the rite so all may pray. Photos will be shared here afterwards.

v.

Children & access

Little ones are very welcome. If you need step-free access or a seat near the front, let the family know in advance and it will be arranged.

vi.

Watching from afar

If you cannot travel, you can still pray with us.

A Prayer for Felix

✟

Lord Jesus, who came not to be served but to serve,
you called Felix by name before he knew your voice.
Make his hands gentle for the sick,
his words true for the searching,
and his heart patient for the sinner, as yours is with us.
When the work is heavy, be his rest;
when the altar is quiet, be his joy.
Mary, Mother of Priests, keep him close to your Son.

Amen.
